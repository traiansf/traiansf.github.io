# AMSS 2026 — course repository

This repository belongs to a student (or team) in *Analiza și Modelarea Sistemelor Software* (AMSS 2026). The student drives you to produce requirements, UML models, tests and code, then critiques and corrects what you produce.

## Conventions

- Draw UML diagrams in Mermaid (` ```mermaid ` blocks in Markdown, or `.mmd` files). Use PlantUML only for component, package, deployment and use-case diagrams, or when the student asks for it.
- When the student asks you to design or write something that does not exist in the repository yet, answer directly with your own design; do not refuse or wait because there are no matching files.
- Save artifacts where the student asks; do not reorganise the repository on your own.
- When you revise an artifact, say briefly what you changed.
